Community Guidelines

Last Updated: 06.11.2026

1. What Kind of Place Should BluuB Be?

BluuB is designed as a community where people can share, through their voices, the thoughts, feelings, and experiences they sometimes find hard to put into words; and where they can listen to other people's posts.

On BluuB, you can share without disclosing your real identity to other users.

However, being anonymous does not give you the right to harm others.

The core principle of the BluuB community is simple:

Be free when telling, be respectful when listening.

These Community Guidelines apply to Bottles, audio responses, messages, usernames, profile information, and other user content on BluuB.

2. You Can Be Yourself

On BluuB, you can be sad, angry, lonely, happy, uncertain, or excited.

You can talk about a relationship, your job, your family, your school, or simply something you experienced that day.

Your content does not need to be positive.

Sharing negative feelings is not against the rules.

BluuB's purpose is not solely to create an artificial environment where only "good things" are told.

However, you must not harm others while expressing yourself.

3. Be Respectful

When communicating with other users;

do not belittle,

do not threaten,

do not systematically harass,

do not bully,

do not insult,

do not force a user who does not want to communicate.

Someone not wanting to talk to you is not a personal attack.

If a user does not accept your communication request, respect their decision.

4. "No" Means No

One of our core principles on BluuB is that users have control over their communications.

Sending a response to a Bottle does not mean that the owner of the Bottle is obliged to talk to you.

If the other party does not accept the communication, do not repeatedly try to communicate.

If a user blocks you;

by opening a new account,

by using a different nickname,

through other users,

by other technical methods

do not try to reach that person again.

This conduct may result in your account being restricted or closed.

5. We Do Not Allow Harassment and Bullying

The following conduct is prohibited:

continuously sending unwanted messages,

demeaning a user,

threatening,

intimidating,

blackmailing,

sexually harassing,

stalking or targeting a user,

inciting other users to attack a person.

Doing this for the purpose of a joke does not automatically make this conduct acceptable.

6. We Do Not Allow Hate Speech

Content directed at a person or group that;

dehumanizes, demeans, or incites violence on the basis of race, ethnicity, nationality, religion, gender, sexual orientation, disability, or similar protected characteristics is not permitted.

Discussing ideas is not the same thing as targeting people because of their identities.

7. Sexual Content and Unwanted Sexual Conduct

Although BluuB is designed for adult users, this does not mean that the Platform is a pornographic or sexual dating platform.

The following are not permitted:

pornographic content,

unwanted sexual messages,

sexual harassment,

persistently requesting sexual content from another user,

sharing non-consensual intimate images or recordings,

trading in or promoting sexual services.

An adult appropriately describing their own experiences regarding their relationship or sexuality is assessed differently from directing sexual content at another user.

8. Child Safety

BluuB is only for persons aged 18 and over.

Content that involves, promotes, or normalizes the sexual abuse or exploitation of children is not permitted under any circumstances.

Such content may be removed, accounts may be permanently closed, and, where applicable law requires, a report may be made to the relevant competent authorities.

9. Violence and Threats

Serious threats of violence directed at real persons are not permitted.

Content threatening to kill, injure, or physically harm a person; that incites violence; or that targets a specific person may be removed.

However, a User describing a bad event they experienced in the past, or expressing their feelings, is not, on its own, prohibited content.

The context of the content is taken into account.

10. Self-Harm and Crisis Content

BluuB allows people to talk about their hard times.

For this reason;

"I feel really bad."

or

"I'm struggling to cope with what I'm going through."

expressions such as these are not removed merely because they are negative.

However, content that promotes self-harm, teaches methods, encourages it, or directs others toward it is not permitted.

BluuB is not a therapy, psychological counseling, or emergency assistance service.

If a User believes that their own life, or that of another person, is under imminent and serious danger, they should seek support from the appropriate emergency services in their location.

11. Do Not Share Other People's Private Information

Belonging to other people;

phone number,

home or work address,

national identity number,

private e-mail address,

financial information,

private correspondence,

intimate photographs or recordings,

location information that could endanger the person's safety

must not be shared without the consent of the person concerned.

We recommend that, when describing an event, you not unnecessarily disclose other people's identities.

For example:

"I had a problem with my manager at work."

to say this, you do not need to give your manager's name, company, and phone number.

12. Do Not Record Other People's Voices Without Consent

An audio recording you upload to BluuB must be one you are authorized to share.

Unlawfully recording other people's private conversations and publishing them on BluuB is prohibited.

When creating a Bottle, be careful that other people's private conversations do not get captured in the background of the recording.

13. Fraud and Requests for Money

You may not use BluuB for the purpose of defrauding users.

In particular;

fake investment offers,

requests to send money,

requesting bank/card information,

fake charity campaigns,

phishing,

account takeover attempts,

directing users to fraud schemes on other platforms

are prohibited.

We recommend that you not send money to users you do not know and that you not share your financial information.

14. Spam and Commercial Use

BluuB is designed for personal sharing and communication purposes.

The following conduct may be considered spam:

sending the same message to a large number of users,

continuously advertising products/services,

engaging in referral/link spam,

using automated accounts,

directing users to other platforms for the purpose of gathering followers or traffic.

Features that BluuB explicitly permits are excluded from this.

15. Fake Identity and Impersonation

You may use a nickname and are not required to show your real name.

However, anonymity is not the same thing as impersonating another person.

Misleading users by pretending to be another real person, company, or organization is prohibited.

16. Usernames Are Also Subject to the Rules

Nicknames;

threats,

hate speech,

severe harassment,

pornographic expression,

impersonating another person's identity,

disclosure of personal information

may not be used for these purposes.

BluuB may change nicknames that violate the rules or restrict the relevant account.

17. Report It

If you encounter a Bottle, message, or user that you believe violates the rules, you can use the Report feature.

Reasons for reporting may, for example, include:

Harassment / Bullying

Hate Speech

Sexual Content

Violence / Threats

Promotion of Self-Harm

Sharing of Personal Information

Fraud / Spam

Suspected Under-18 User

Other

may be classified as such.

Reporting does not automatically mean that the other party's account will be closed.

A report is evaluated within the framework of the applicable rules and available information.

18. Block

If you no longer want to communicate with a User, you can use the Block feature.

A blocked user cannot, to the extent possible, establish new communication with you.

You do not need the other user to have violated the rules in order to use the block feature.

Not wanting to talk to someone is sufficient reason to block them.

19. Moderation

BluuB may carry out moderation actions regarding user content and accounts for the purpose of ensuring safety.

If the rules are violated;

content may be removed,

the visibility of content may be reduced,

certain features may be restricted,

a warning may be given,

the account may be temporarily suspended,

the account may be permanently closed.

The measure applied may vary depending on the nature and severity of the violation, its repetition, and its effect on user safety.

20. Automated Systems

BluuB may make use of automated systems in the future for security and moderation purposes.

These systems may, for example;

help identify signals relating to spam, fraud, threats, inappropriate content, or misuse of the Platform.

Where automated systems are used, their effects on data protection and user rights are separately assessed.

21. Do Not Try to Circumvent the Rules

For the purpose of circumventing a sanction;

opening a new account,

using another user's account,

bypassing technical security measures,

engaging in manipulation

is prohibited.

This conduct may result in other related accounts also being restricted.

22. BluuB's Core Principles

We do not expect you to remember all of the lengthy rules.

It is enough to remember these five principles while using BluuB:

1. Be free when telling.

2. Be respectful when listening.

3. Do not use anonymity to harm others.

4. Respect the other party's decision if they do not want to communicate.

5. If you see something dangerous or inappropriate, report it.
