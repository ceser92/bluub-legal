When the filter is used, the listener may indirectly learn the gender declared by the owner of the Bottle found.

The User may optionally add a profile photo and an "About" text. A profile photo is not shown to other users until it is approved; it is reviewed by an authorised moderator and may go through an automated safety check to assist that review.

5. Important Explanation Regarding Voice Data

Your voice, by its nature, may carry characteristics capable of distinguishing you as a person.

However, the purpose of BluuB's core voice-sharing feature is not to detect or verify the User's biometric identity.

Audio recordings will not be used by BluuB for the purpose of voice biometrics, face/voice matching, or uniquely verifying a person's identity, unless such a feature is separately and explicitly developed and the necessary legal processes are completed.

Audio recordings are processed primarily for the purpose of presenting the User's created content to other users and operating the Platform's features.

We recommend that you do not share special categories of personal data, such as information about your health, sex life, religion or political opinions, in your Bottles or messages. If you choose to share such information, it is processed only for the sharing you chose (playing it to other users), for review before publication and for reviewing reports; BluuB does not separately extract or classify it or use it to build a profile about you.

Audio recordings are not analysed to build a profile of the User's health, emotional state or mood. However, for community safety, Bottles submitted for publication are not shown to other users until they are approved, and authorised moderators listen to and review them against the Community Guidelines. For this review, Bottle audio is not sent to any automated speech-to-text or content classification service. The review includes paying attention to urgent risk statements such as self-harm; this is used only for the moderation decision and, where needed, to point to support resources. Profile photos may be checked automatically with the OpenAI (USA) moderation service to assist the moderator's review. The review outcome is stored as the moderation record.

If the User uses the AI voice option, the recording is first converted into text: on Android by the on-device Vosk model (downloaded from alphacephei.com on first use), on iOS by Apple's speech recognition service (on the device where supported, otherwise on Apple's servers). The text the User approves is spoken by the text-to-speech engine on the device. AI voice generation is available to Premium members; to count allowances, the time and outcome of each attempt are recorded on the server, and the text and recording are not sent to the server for this count.

8. Device and Technical Information

For the purpose of BluuB operating securely and properly, the following technical information may be processed:

IP address,

device type,

operating system,

app version,

device/app identifiers,

language and region information,

session information,

connection information,

error and crash logs,

security logs,

app performance information.

This data may be used for the purposes of running the app, detecting errors, ensuring security, and improving service quality.

When a guest session is created, an account is registered, or a sign-in is attempted, the IP address associated with the network connection may be used temporarily in a security check to limit abuse, automated requests, password guessing, and repeated free-entitlement creation. Instead of storing the IP address itself in the rate-limit event database, this limiter derives an HMAC-based pseudonymous network identifier using a server-side secret key. This network identifier is used only for Platform security, abuse prevention, and rate limiting. Under this mechanism, the raw IP address, IP-prefix bytes, and HMAC input are not stored in the event table or logged. Although it is not the raw IP address, the HMAC network identifier is pseudonymous personal data.

After a successful password sign-in, a server-keyed pseudonymous value of the random installation ID that the app creates on the device is kept as the account's "known" device for 30 days from the last successful sign-in; this record is used only to stop other people's failed sign-in attempts from blocking the account holder signing in from their own device. The network used to sign in is not stored as "known".

When the "Remember me" option is on, only your username is stored on this device and pre-filled at your next sign-in. Your password, session keys or Google/Apple details are not stored for this purpose; a password or provider verification is required again after every sign-out. When you turn the option off or submit an account deletion request, the stored username is removed from the device.

17. How Long Is Personal Data Retained?

Personal data is retained for as long as required by the relevant processing purpose, taking into account the retention obligations set out in applicable legislation. Retention periods may vary depending on the data category, the purpose of processing, security needs, and legal obligations.

Personal data for which the retention period has ended or the processing purpose has ceased to exist is, in accordance with applicable legislation:

deleted, destroyed, or anonymized.

The retention periods applied by data type are as follows:

Account data → for as long as the account is active; after a deletion request, for the period for withdrawing the deletion request (see Section 18), followed by permanent deletion by the daily clean-up job. Limited records that must be kept by law are excluded.

Encrypted database and file backups → no more than 7 days. Backups are kept, encrypted, only on a computer under the operator's control and, optionally, in the operator's cloud storage account. The hosting plan used does not include automatic database backups by the provider (Supabase).

Deleted Bottles → removal following the technical deletion/backup period.

Security records → the limited period required for security and legal requests.

Provider access logs → Our hosting and network providers (Supabase, Cloudflare) keep access logs, which may include IP addresses, for their own retention periods for the security of the service. BluuB's own application tables do not store raw IP addresses; the authentication service may keep IP addresses in sign-in and session records for session security.

Complaint/moderation records → the period required by the dispute and security need.

Guest-session and registration rate-limit events → a target of 48 hours and, in all cases, no more than 72 hours.

Sign-in attempt rate-limit events → 48 hours (with hourly clean-up).

Known device records → 30 days from the last successful sign-in.

Legal-hold records → for as long as the relevant legal obligation or dispute continues; a separate maximum period has not yet been set.

18. What Happens When an Account Is Deleted?

A User may request that their account be deleted.

After a deletion request, the account is kept in a "pending deletion" state for 180 days; this is the period for withdrawing the deletion request, during which the User can sign in and explicitly cancel it. If it is not cancelled, permanent deletion starts when the period ends; stored Bottle and message audio files and support request attachments are also deleted in this process. A deletion request can be submitted only once every 30 days; cancelling does not reset this period. Pending requests created before this rule took effect remain subject to the period communicated when they were made. Because a conversation belongs to both participants, when either participant's account is permanently deleted the conversation is deleted for both.

When the account deletion process is carried out, personal data relating to the User;

except for information that must be legally retained, ongoing legal disputes, and limited records mandatory for security and the prevention of misuse

is subject to the relevant retention and destruction processes.

It may not be technically possible for data to be physically destroyed from all backup systems in the same second as the account is deleted. Data contained in backups is removed within the determined backup/destruction cycle.

20. Your Rights Under the KVKK

Under Article 11 of the KVKK, by applying to the data controller with respect to your personal data, you have the right to;

a) learn whether your personal data is being processed,

b) request information regarding your personal data, if it has been processed,

c) learn the purpose of the processing of your personal data and whether it is used in accordance with that purpose,

d) know the third parties, within Turkey or abroad, to whom your personal data is transferred,

e) request the correction of your personal data if it has been processed incompletely or incorrectly,

f) request the deletion or destruction of your personal data within the framework of the conditions set out in the KVKK,

g) request that the correction or deletion/destruction actions be notified to the third parties to whom your personal data has been transferred,

h) object to the emergence of a result to your own detriment through the exclusively automated analysis of processed data,

i) request compensation for damage arising from the unlawful processing of your personal data,

as your rights.

These rights are set out in the current Article 11 of the KVKK.

21. How to Submit a KVKK Application?

You may submit your requests under the KVKK to the data controller, in Turkish and in accordance with the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller, by one of the following means:

by e-mail to bluubapp@gmail.com from the e-mail address registered with BluuB (for accounts opened with Google, the registered address is your Google account's e-mail address; for accounts opened with a username, it is the recovery e-mail address you verified),

by sending your application signed with a secure electronic signature or mobile signature to bluubapp@gmail.com,

after signing in to the BluuB app, through the Support form in Settings, choosing the "Account issue" category and stating that your request is made under the KVKK.

Under the Communiqué, the application contains your name, surname and, if made in writing, your signature; for citizens of the Republic of Türkiye, the T.R. identity number, and for foreigners, nationality and passport number or, if any, identity number; the residential or business address for notifications; if any, the e-mail address, telephone or fax number for notifications; and the subject of the request. Please also include your username so that we can find your BluuB account.

Your application is concluded free of charge as soon as possible according to the nature of the request and at the latest within thirty days. If the process requires an additional cost, a fee may be charged according to the tariff set by the Personal Data Protection Board.

BluuB may request additional information appropriate and proportionate to the nature of the application in order to verify identity.

Where the application to the data controller is rejected, found insufficient, or not answered within the period set out in the KVKK, the data subject may lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Board within thirty days from the date on which they become aware of this situation, and, in any event, within sixty days from the date of the application.

Under Article 13 of the KVKK, applying to the data controller is a precondition for lodging a complaint with the Board; the exceptional circumstances set out in the Law are reserved.
