BluuB uses microphone access only within the framework of the permissions granted by the operating system.

AI voice option: if you choose to share your Bottle in an AI voice instead of your own voice, your recording is first converted into text. On Android this is done on your device by the Vosk speech recognition model; the model is downloaded to your device from alphacephei.com on first use (during the download the download server can see your device's IP address; your recording is not sent to it). On iOS, Apple's speech recognition service is used; if your device supports it, recognition runs on the device, otherwise the recording may be processed on Apple's servers. You see and can correct the resulting text before publishing; the text you approve is spoken by the text-to-speech engine on your device, and other users hear that AI voice. AI voice generation is available to Premium members. To count allowances, the time and outcome (successful/failed) of each generation attempt are recorded on our servers; your text and recording are not sent to the server for this count. If you send the Bottle, only the resulting AI voice is uploaded.

8. Technical and Device Information

When you use BluuB, certain technical information may be automatically generated.

These;

IP address,

operating system,

device type,

app version,

language and region,

connection information,

error and crash logs,

session information,

app or device identifiers,

security logs

may include information such as the above.

This data may be used for the purposes of running the app, resolving technical issues, and improving security and performance.

When a guest session is created, an account is registered, or a sign-in is attempted, the IP address associated with the network connection may be used temporarily in a security check to limit abuse, automated requests, password guessing, and repeated free-entitlement creation. Instead of storing the IP address itself in the rate-limit event database, this limiter derives an HMAC-based pseudonymous network identifier using a server-side secret key. The identifier is used only for security, abuse prevention, and rate limiting. Under this mechanism, the raw IP address, IP-prefix bytes, and HMAC input are not stored in the event table or logged. Although it is not the raw IP address, the HMAC network identifier is pseudonymous technical data that may constitute personal data.

After a successful password sign-in to your account, a server-keyed pseudonymous value of the random installation ID that the app creates on your device is kept as your account's "known" device for 30 days from the last successful sign-in. This record is used only to stop other people's failed sign-in attempts against your account from blocking you when you sign in from your own device. The network you sign in from is not stored as "known".

When the "Remember me" option is on, only your username is stored on this device and pre-filled at your next sign-in. Your password, session keys or Google/Apple details are not stored for this purpose; a password or provider verification is required again after every sign-out. When you turn the option off or submit an account deletion request, the stored username is removed from the device.

Our server requests pass through the Cloudflare network layer used by our infrastructure provider; the IP address used in security checks is read from the information forwarded by that layer.

9. Usage and Analytics Information

The BluuB app does not contain a separate analytics (user behaviour tracking) SDK.

To run the service, the following usage information is kept on our servers: the start time of your account's fixed 24-hour allowance period (the moment your account was created); listening, Bottle publishing, AI voice generation and AI-voice Bottle sending allowances in these periods and their use; the number of chats started with new people in a period and which people you have already started a chat with (to apply the new-contact limit), the Bottles you listened to and your listening history, listen counts of Bottles, and rewarded-ad viewing and completion records.

In the release version of the app and when Firebase is configured, if the app crashes or a serious error occurs, an error report (including technical details such as device model, operating system and app version) is sent automatically through Firebase Crashlytics (Google). No user identifier is added to this report; error messages caught by the app's own layer are shortened before sending and parts that may be personal, such as emails, tokens, links and identifiers, are removed. Crashes in Android's native layer are captured by Crashlytics directly and do not go through this filtering; these reports contain the technical stack trace and device details. There is currently no separate in-app setting to turn this reporting off.

19. How Can You Delete Your Account?

You can delete your BluuB account from the account settings within the app. You can also initiate your account deletion request from https://ceser92.github.io/bluub-legal/en/account-deletion/.

After a deletion request, your account is kept in a "pending deletion" state for 180 days; this is the period for withdrawing your deletion request, during which you can sign in and explicitly cancel it. If you do not cancel it, your account and related data enter permanent deletion when the period ends; stored Bottle and message audio files and support request attachments are also deleted in this process. You can submit a deletion request only once every 30 days; cancelling a request does not reset this period. Pending requests created before this rule took effect remain subject to the period communicated when they were made. Because a conversation belongs to both participants, when either participant's account is permanently deleted the conversation is deleted for both.

Following account deletion, personal data and user content associated with your account are subject to the applicable retention and destruction processes, except for records that must be legally retained or that must be retained for a limited period for security/dispute purposes.

Certain data may need to be retained for a limited period for security, legal dispute, or legal obligation reasons.
