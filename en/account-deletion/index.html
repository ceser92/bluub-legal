Account Deletion

Last Updated: 06.11.2026
This is the official account deletion page of the BluuB mobile app. Operator and data controller (natural person): Caner Eser · bluubapp@gmail.com

1. Deleting In the App (recommended)

The quickest way is to delete your account directly in the app:

Open the BluuB app → sign in → go to Settings → Delete My Account → verify your identity again with your password or with Google/Apple → confirm.

2. Requesting Deletion Without Using the App

If you do not want to keep the app installed, you can also request the deletion of your account from this page. When you tap the button below, your device's email app opens with a pre-filled "account deletion request" email to BluuB's official address.

Send Account Deletion Request

Your request is processed after we verify your identity. You can also write to the same address (bluubapp@gmail.com) by other means.

3. What Happens After the Request?

Your account will be closed and kept for 180 days before deletion. This is the period for withdrawing your deletion request: during it, you can sign in and cancel the request. If you do not cancel it, your account will be permanently deleted after this period. You can submit a deletion request only once every 30 days.

While pending, your account is hidden from other users and you cannot create new Bottles, replies or messages. Cancelling a request does not reset the 30-day request limit. Pending requests created before this rule took effect remain subject to the period communicated when they were made.

4. Which Data Is Deleted?

At the end of the 180-day period, your account/profile information, your Bottles and audio recordings, your conversation/message history and your notification preferences are permanently removed from user access in the app. Because a conversation belongs to both participants, the conversation is deleted for both.

5. Records That May Be Kept for a Limited Time

Limited technical records that must be kept because of a legal obligation, an ongoing security incident, a complaint/moderation process or a dispute (e.g. username, account creation/deletion dates, security logs) may be kept separately in an access-controlled area, only for this purpose and for a limited time. These records are not publicly linked to any profile in the app and are not used for product analytics.

6. Questions

For questions about account deletion, contact us at bluubapp@gmail.com.
