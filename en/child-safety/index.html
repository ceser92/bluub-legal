Child Safety Standards

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This page describes BluuB's child safety standards.

Age restriction

BluuB is intended only for people aged 18 and over. Users are asked to confirm their age when registering. Accounts suspected to belong to minors may be reviewed and restricted.

Zero tolerance

Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), sexualization of children, grooming, and attempts to exploit or abuse children through the service are strictly prohibited.

Moderation and enforcement

Voice bottles are subject to moderation review before they are made available to other users. Suspected violations are reviewed; confirmed prohibited content may be removed and associated accounts restricted or terminated.

Reporting and blocking

Users can report inappropriate bottles, profiles and chat messages in the app, and can block other users. Child-safety reports are prioritized for review.

Legal compliance and reporting

BluuB aims to comply with applicable child-safety laws, cooperate with competent authorities when required, and make legally required reports. Suspected material and associated records are handled in accordance with applicable laws and data-protection requirements.

Contact

Child-safety point of contact: Caner Eser. Send reports or concerns to bluubapp@gmail.com. If someone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or law enforcement.
