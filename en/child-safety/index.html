Child Safety Standards

Last Updated: 11.11.2026

This page describes the standards the BluuB mobile app (Google Play package name: app.bluub) applies against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). Operator: Caner Eser.

1. Age Restriction

BluuB is only for people aged 18 and over. Users must separately confirm that they are 18 or older when registering (by email or with Google) and before continuing as a guest. Accounts suspected to belong to someone under 18 are reviewed and may be restricted or closed.

2. Zero Tolerance

Child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), any content that sexualises children, and any communication intended to groom, deceive or exploit a child are strictly prohibited.

3. Review Before Publication

Voice bottles are checked before they are shown to other users. Bottles flagged by the automatic check, or whose check cannot be completed, are not published until a moderator reviews them.

4. Reporting and Blocking

Users can report bottles, profiles and chat messages in the app and can block other users. Reports of sexual content are reviewed with critical priority. To report a concern about a child's safety, choose "Sexual content" on the report screen and describe it in the description field, or write to the email address below.

5. Enforcement

Content confirmed to violate these child safety rules is removed, and the account concerned is restricted or closed.

6. Legal Compliance and Authorities

BluuB is committed to complying with applicable child safety laws and to cooperating with competent authorities where required. Suspected material and related records are handled in accordance with applicable law and personal data protection rules.

7. Contact

Child safety point of contact: Caner Eser. Send reports and questions to bluubapp@gmail.com. If a child is in immediate danger, first contact local emergency services or law enforcement.
